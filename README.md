# 🎒 Wawapp — Impact Technology for Child Safety and Autonomy

> **Wawapp** is a social impact and civic technology platform that transforms a child's daily route (school ↔ home) into an interactive 3D playful experience based on the real urban environment from **OpenStreetMap**. It is not a passive tracker: it is a tool for **spatial literacy, resilience, and self-care** that trains children to know **what to do when the plan changes**.

## 💡 The "Why": Origin and Social Purpose

### 1. The Social Reality in Latin America
In Peru and much of Latin America, millions of single mothers and working families face a daily dilemma: supporting the household economically while also being physically present for every school dismissal time.

The project was born from a real case in **San Juan de Lurigancho (Lima)**: a 7-year-old boy waited for his mother at the school gate for more than an hour due to a mix-up in her work schedule. The child did not know whether to walk alone, stay put, or whom to talk to. **The danger was not only the distance home; the real danger was the panic and disorientation when the routine failed.**

### 2. Why Not a "Passive GPS Tracker"
Commercial parental control apps usually limit themselves to turning the child into a passive dot on a screen. This creates three serious deficiencies:
1. **It teaches the child nothing:** If the phone loses signal, turns off, or the child gets confused about the street, the child remains cognitively unprotected.
2. **Privacy risk:** Centralizing the exact coordinates of minors' routes on cloud servers creates an unacceptable attack vector and data leak risk.
3. **Anxiety instead of autonomy:** Safety does not come from constant surveillance, but from **giving the child judgment, spatial memory, and emotional calm**.

**Wawapp changes the paradigm:** it replaces passive surveillance with **active spatial learning and playful psychological preparation.**

## 🧠 The Why Behind Every Design Decision

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ MOM MODE │
│ Route design · Trust landmarks · Child's name │
└──────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────┘
│ Payload link (URL Hash)
▼
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ CHILD MODE │
│ 3D Avatar · OSM Urban Twin · Questions · Confetti │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 1. Why a Real OpenStreetMap 3D Digital Twin?
At ages 6–11, children struggle to abstract a top-down 2D map (viewed from the sky). However, they immediately recognize **three-dimensional visual anchor points**: the corner store with bars, the illuminated pharmacy, the height of neighboring houses, and the pedestrian traffic light.
* Using **Three.js** and the **OpenStreetMap Overpass API**, Wawapp projects the real buildings with their estimated heights and the exact layout of local streets.
* The child explores their own neighborhood virtually before walking it in real life.

### 2. Why the Dual-Role Architecture (Mom vs. Child)?
* **Mom Mode (Safe Planning):**
  * The mother defines the safe corridor: sets the **Starting Point** (🏫 School), marks the **Path** (👣 numbered footprints), and sets the **Arrival** (🏠 Home / 👵 Grandma's House).
  * Adds **Neighborhood Support Points**: friendly corner stores, pharmacies with identified staff, family parks, and crossings with traffic lights.
  * Personalizes her child's name to provide closeness and affection.
* **Child Mode (Gamified Adventure):**
  * Accessed through a unique **direct link** generated by the mother.
  * All editing bars or technical settings are removed.
  * The child is greeted with their own name (`🎒 Hi, Thiago!`) and takes on the route as a mission where they earn stars and learn to take care of themselves.

### 3. Why a Shareable Link with No Registration or Central Server?
* **Absolute Privacy (Zero-Knowledge):** The route, points, and name are serialized and encoded in the URL hash fragment (`#data=...`) and in local storage (`localStorage`).
* **Zero access barriers:** The child does not need to create accounts, remember passwords, or install heavy apps; they simply open the link mom sent via WhatsApp and the app works in any mobile or desktop browser.
* **No server collects the location or names of minors.**

### 4. Why a Visible and Articulated 3D Chibi Character?
* Children need an **avatar they can emotionally identify with**.
* The character has sneakers, cheerful sportswear, a kids' cap, and their **school backpack**.
* It includes a fluid walking cycle (alternating arm movement, leg strides, and backpack sway) with a floating name tag.
* The camera follows in a **close and immersive third-person view**, allowing the child to see their character advance step by step along the sidewalks.

### 5. Why Situational Questions of Real Value?
Instead of generic trivia, the pedagogical bank (`src/questions.js`) trains **urban survival protocols**:
* **School dismissal:** What to firmly answer if a stranger says *"your mom sent me to pick you up"* and the importance of staying with the teacher.
* **Pedestrian crossing:** Checking that cars have stopped before stepping on the crosswalk, even with a green light.
* **Reaction to unexpected events:** If a ball rolls onto the street, **do not chase it** and ask an adult for help.
* **Community trust network:** Going to the friendly corner store or the illuminated pharmacy to ask for a phone call to mom if they feel disoriented or intimidated.

### 6. Why Confetti and Sounds on Correct Answers?
* In child psychology, **immediate positive reinforcement** triggers dopaminergic responses that consolidate long-term memory.
* A native Canvas particle engine was implemented (`src/confetti.js`) with explosions of stars, streamers, and multicolored confetti, accompanied by a harmonic fanfare via the **Web Audio API** (100% native, no external dependencies or downloads).
* The 3D character performs a celebration jump with arms raised.

### 7. Why a "Sublime" and Kind Orientation When Failing?
* **In emergency situations, fear blocks thinking.** Punishing the error with harsh sounds or red crosses generates frustration and anxiety.
* If the child makes a mistake, Wawapp emits a soft and welcoming tone, highlights the safe option in green, and displays a constructive and affectionate explanation:
> *"💡 Good try, champion! Let's learn together: Cars don't always see a child bending down in the street; that's why we always stay on the sidewalk and ask an adult for help."*
* This fosters a continuous learning mindset and internal safety.

### 8. Why the "SOS / I'm Lost" Button?
* If the child is on the street and feels lost, the red emergency button:
  1. Uses the device's geolocation.
  2. Calculates via the Haversine formula which are the **3 nearest safe points or businesses** that their mother mapped.
  3. Prepares an instant WhatsApp message with the real-time GPS location on Google Maps and the safe place they are heading to, ready to send to mom with a single tap.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack and Architecture

| Component                 | Technology                                                                                                         | Reason for Choice                                                               |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **3D Engine**             | [Three.js r160](https://threejs.org/)                                                                              | Hardware-accelerated WebGL rendering without plugins.                           |
| **2D Cartography**        | [Leaflet 1.9.4](https://leafletjs.com/)                                                                            | Lightweight, tactile, and high-performance on mobile devices.                   |
| **Geospatial Data**       | [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/) + [Overpass API](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Overpass_API) | Open and collaborative urban data at a global level.                            |
| **Audio Synthesizer**     | Native Web Audio API                                                                                               | Procedural generation of frequencies and bells without external audio files.    |
| **Particle Physics**      | Native Canvas 2D                                                                                                   | Confetti at 60 FPS with gravity, air resistance, and shape rotation.            |
| **Bundler**               | [Vite 5.4](https://vitejs.dev/)                                                                                    | Ultra-fast compilation times and instant hot reload.                            |
| **Children's Typography** | Google Fonts (Fredoka & Nunito)                                                                                    | Proven readability and aesthetic warmth for minors.                             |

## 🚀 Installation and Getting Started

### Prerequisites
* Node.js v18.0.0 or higher
* Browser with WebGL support enabled (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox)

### Local Execution
```bash
# 1. Clone the repository or enter the folder
cd wawapp

# 2. Install dependencies
npm install

# 3. Start the live development server
npm run dev
```
Open in your browser: `http://localhost:5173/`

### Generate optimized production version
```bash
npm run build
npm run preview
```

### Pre-download an OpenStreetMap quadrant (Offline Mode)
```bash
# Default download (example zone in San Juan de Lurigancho)
npm run osm

# Or specifying a custom Bounding Box [south west north east]:
npm run osm -- -11.9935 -77.0105 -11.9880 -77.0040
```

## 🔒 Privacy and Project Ethics

* **Ethical Design by Default:** Wawapp contains no advertising, commercial telemetry trackers, or invasive cookies.
* **No Centralized Database of Minors:** Routes never reside on a central server. The information belongs exclusively to the mother and her child.
* **Right to Child Self-Care:** Active education is encouraged instead of restrictive hyper-surveillance.
